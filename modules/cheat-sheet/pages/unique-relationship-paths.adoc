====
include::ROOT:patterns/unique-relationship-paths.adoc[tag=patterns_different_relationships]

[.description]
By default, Cypher only prevents relationships being repeated in a `MATCH` result, not nodes.
This behavior can be explicitly defined by adding the `DIFFERENT RELATIONSHIPS` keyword after `MATCH`, though this does not alter the rules how patterns are matched in Cypher.
